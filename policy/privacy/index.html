Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 Oct 2026

This policy explains how DumaBoost (Duma Brand) collects and uses personal data under Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. [Personal Data Protection Commission registration number to be inserted after registration.]

What we collect

  • Phone number (for payment, order updates and tracking).
  • Email and name, if you choose to give them.
  • Links and details you enter for an order.
  • Payment information from our payment provider (status, amount, network). We never receive or store PINs, passwords or full card numbers.
  • Technical data (IP address, browser) for security and fraud prevention.

How we use it

To deliver your order, confirm payment, send order updates, answer support requests, prevent fraud and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

Our payment provider, the providers who fulfil your order (only what they need), email/SMS providers, and authorities where the law requires. Some providers may store data outside Tanzania; we put the legally required safeguards in place. [Processor list and countries to be added.]

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept as long as tax and commercial law require [period to be confirmed by accountant/lawyer]. Support messages are kept up to 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to certain uses, by contacting support@dumaboost.com.
