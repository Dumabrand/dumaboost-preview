Help centre

Quick answers. Can’t find yours? Our team replies on working days.

Payments

How do I pay? +

Pick M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mixx by Yas or HaloPesa and enter your number. A prompt pops up on your phone — enter your PIN. Done. Cards open a secure payment page.

The payment prompt did not appear. What now? +

Check that your phone is on and has network, then tap “Send prompt again” on the payment page. You have not been charged unless you entered your PIN.

The PIN prompt did not show up. +

Make sure your phone is on and has network, then tap "Send prompt again". You are not charged unless you enter your PIN.

Money left my account but the order says unpaid. +

Some networks confirm late. We keep checking for 48 hours and your order updates by itself. Still stuck after 30 minutes? Contact us with your order number.

Delivery

My followers dropped. What can I do? +

Some services include refill — the service page says so. Contact support with your order number within the refill period and we will request it from the provider.

How fast is delivery? +

Most packages start within minutes to a few hours. Each package shows its usual time.

How do I follow my order? +

After paying you get a tracking link. You can also open “Track order” and enter your order number with the phone number you paid with.

My followers dropped. What now? +

Some packages include refill — the package page says so. Contact us with your order number and we will request it.

Refunds

Can I get a refund? +

Yes, when we cannot deliver what you ordered. Each service page explains its refund rules. Refunds go back to the number or card you paid with.

Can I get my money back? +

Yes, for anything we could not deliver. It goes back to the number or card you paid with.

About DumaBoost

Do you sell followers or likes? +

No. Fake engagement breaks TikTok, Instagram and Facebook rules and can get accounts restricted. We offer official ads, real creators and quality content instead — the things that bring real customers.

Are the followers and likes real people? +

Not always. Packages come from bulk providers and many accounts are inactive or automated. Numbers can drop and apps may remove them. For real customers, try an ad campaign or a creator post.

Do you need my password? +

Never. Only a public link. Anyone asking for your password or code in our name is not us.

Will you need my password? +

Never. We only need public links or files you share. Anyone asking for your password or verification code in our name is not us.
