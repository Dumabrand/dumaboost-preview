Ways to earn more

  • Raise markup a little on your best sellers — small packages carry the highest % margin.
  • Set a minimum profit per order (e.g. TSh 300) so tiny orders are never worth less than the effort.
  • Re-sync supplier prices weekly; if the panel gets cheaper, your margin grows.
  • Keep refunds low: pick panels with refill and low drop rates.
